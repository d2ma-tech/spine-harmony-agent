## Example agent summary

The diary shows a low-pain trend after the initial entry, interrupted by one acute sitting-triggered flare. The useful clinician-facing framing is: “overall improvement with one clear activity-triggered setback,” not “the intervention definitely worked.” Continue preserving raw pain scores, activity context, medication use, and any new symptoms for follow-up discussion.

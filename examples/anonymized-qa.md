## User question

The imaging summary says there is "degenerative change at a lower lumbar level." What should I ask the specialist so I understand whether that finding matters?

## Spine Harmony-style answer

The phrase is broad and does not, by itself, prove that the imaging finding is the source of pain. A useful consultation goal is to connect three things:

1. the exact imaging level and finding;
2. the symptom pattern;
3. the clinician's physical-exam interpretation.

Questions to ask:

- Which specific level and structure does the imaging finding refer to?
- Does that finding match my symptoms and examination, or could it be incidental?
- Are there signs of nerve involvement, instability, inflammatory change, or another pain generator?
- What conservative options remain reasonable before considering invasive treatment?
- What symptoms would require urgent review?

This is for consultation preparation only and is not a diagnosis or treatment recommendation.

For severe, new, progressive, or concerning symptoms — including neurological deficits, progressive weakness, bladder or bowel changes, saddle anesthesia, fever with spinal pain, trauma, suspected infection, or any urgent red flag — seek immediate medical care or emergency services. Do not wait for an AI response.

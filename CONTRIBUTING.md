# Contributing

Contributions are welcome if they preserve the project's spine-specific, clinically cautious, privacy-conscious boundaries.

## Do not submit private data

Do not submit real patient data, medical records, transcripts, imaging, converted images, metadata dumps, credentials, or private identifiers.

## Contributor medical disclaimer

Contributions to this repository are not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment guidance, emergency triage, radiology interpretation, clinical decision support, or professional healthcare services. By contributing, you are not creating a clinician-patient relationship with users and you are not assuming responsibility for downstream users' medical decisions, AI/runtime configuration, hosted deployments, or reliance on outputs.

## Safety requirements for contributions

Contributions must:

- preserve clear no-medical-advice/no-diagnosis/no-treatment/no-emergency-triage language;
- avoid claims that Spine Harmony is safe, validated, clinically reliable, regulator-approved, or suitable for unsupervised clinical use;
- avoid instructing users to start/stop medication, delay care, ignore symptoms, accept/reject surgery, or override clinicians;
- keep imaging language framed as non-clinical organization and clinician-question preparation;
- keep examples synthetic unless legal/privacy review explicitly approves otherwise.

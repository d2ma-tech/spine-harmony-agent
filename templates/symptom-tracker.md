## Check-in prompt templates

### Morning

1. Pain level on waking, 0–10?
2. How did you sleep?
3. Any stiffness, spasms, flares, or new symptoms?
4. Any medication taken since last check-in?
5. Main activity goal today?

### Midday / activity check

1. Pain level right now, 0–10?
2. Activity so far: sitting, standing, walking, work, therapy, exercise, travel?
3. Any flare, spasm, acute episode, or new symptom since the last entry?
4. Medication or comfort measures used today?
5. Is today better, same, worse, or mixed compared with the prior entry?

### Evening

1. Pain level right now, 0–10?
2. How did the day go overall?
3. Activity summary?
4. Medication used today — type and amount if the user chooses to record it?
5. Heat/ice/rest/position changes or other comfort measures?
6. Any flares, spasms, acute episodes, or new symptoms?
7. Compared with yesterday: better, same, worse, or mixed?
8. Any questions this raises for the clinician?

## Trend interpretation guardrails

- Preserve raw user-reported facts.
- Do not overstate improvement, deterioration, treatment success, or treatment failure.
- Separate “reported trend” from “clinical meaning.”
- Bring red-line or new neurological symptoms to a qualified clinician promptly.
- Use trends to prepare better clinician questions, not to self-diagnose or self-treat.
